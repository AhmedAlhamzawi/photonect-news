### A generator bug worth keeping
`gen_scenes_2026_09_23.py` called `first_image_url(info)` with the **full response envelope**; that
helper only matches inside `data{}`, and its `walk()` fallback never inspects strings *inside a list*,
which is exactly where `resultUrls` lives. Every one of the 20 tasks **succeeded and was billed**, but
nothing downloaded and the script sat in its poll loop. Because the task IDs were persisted first
(`scripts/_kie_jobs_2026_09_23.json`), `scripts/dl_kie_2026_09_23.py` collected all 20 with no
re-submission and no extra spend. **Pass `info["data"]`, not `info`.**
