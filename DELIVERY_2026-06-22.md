## Story signals & sources
1. **iraq-worldcup-france** — Iraq returns to the FIFA World Cup for the first time since **1986**, opening today against **France** in Group I (with Senegal and Norway). Iraq sealed the **48th** and final berth by beating Bolivia in the inter-confederation playoff in Monterrey, Mexico; in the expanded format the best third-placed teams reach the round of 32. *(Reel is result-agnostic — no score declared.)* Sources: FIFA, Inside FIFA, ESPN, Wikipedia, Tribuna.
2. **iran-us-switzerland-talks** — US VP **JD Vance** arrived in Switzerland on **21 June** for talks with Iran; FM **Abbas Araghchi** reported "major progress" toward ending the Lebanon war. The sides agreed to a **de-confliction cell** with Lebanon to halt military operations, with Iran's oil-export sanctions waived and some frozen assets released, under **Qatar and Pakistan** mediation. Syria's President Ahmed al-Sharaa said Damascus won't fight Hezbollah — a de-escalation that lowers risk to Iraq's stability. Sources: Reuters, Times of Israel, Al Jazeera, CNN, Profile News.
3. **iraq-militia-disarmament** — On **2 June**, Asaib Ahl al-Haq and Kataib Imam Ali pledged to put their arms under state control and sever ties with the Popular Mobilization Forces; PM **Ali al-Zaidi** met faction delegations on **3 June**. Baghdad set end-**September** as the deadline under US pressure and linked it to the international coalition's withdrawal, while Harakat Hezbollah al-Nujaba rejected the initiative — leaving implementation uncertain. Sources: Long War Journal, FDD, Arab News, The National, Amwaj.
4. **gcc-grand-tours-visa** — The six GCC states are launching a Schengen-style unified "Grand Tours" tourist visa (pilot late 2026; **$100–150**; **30–90** days multi-entry across all members). The **2,117 km** GCC Railway is ~**50%** complete and the UAE's Etihad Rail has begun **900 km** of passenger service; Gulf aviation has recovered to ~**85–90%** of capacity — a transport/integration push, not an energy story. Sources: Travel And Tour World, Gulf News, Etihad Rail, Gulf Nashra, GCC.
5. **pancreas-cancer-pill** — The Phase 3 **RASolute 302** trial showed the oral RAS(ON) inhibitor **daraxonrasib** raised median overall survival to **13.2** months vs **6.7** for chemotherapy (HR **0.40**, p<**0.0001**) in previously-treated metastatic RAS-mutated pancreatic cancer — the first RAS inhibitor in a large randomized pancreatic-cancer trial, taken as a once-daily pill. Presented at ASCO on **31 May** and published in NEJM. Sources: NEJM, Dana-Farber, Revolution Medicines, OncLive, ASCO.
6. **coral-resilient-reefs** — Scientists (Wildlife Conservation Society + Macquarie University) mapped climate-resilient coral reefs covering **165,922 km²** across **71** countries — three times prior estimates. Between **2023–2025**, heat stress hit ~**84%** of the world's reef area in **83** countries (the fourth global mass bleaching); **61%** of resilient refuges sit in five countries, and NOAA expects El Niño to intensify. Sources: Wildlife Conservation Society, Macquarie University, CBC, NOAA, Earth.org.
