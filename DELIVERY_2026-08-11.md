## Directional shift vs 2026-08-10

1. **First slate to lead on the wage bill itself rather than a service failure.** Every recent lead
   opened on something breaking in the physical world — a blackout decision, burning tyres, a cut
   to Basra's share. Today's lead is a balance sheet: 7.8 trillion needed, 3.2 announced, forty
   days elapsed. It tests whether the audience will sit for a fiscal reel when the stake is their
   own payslip, and it carries the adviser's reassurance rather than editorialising against it.
2. **A new pillar: the consumer durable.** Slot 4 is the first reel to price a *purchase decision*
   rather than a bill or a rate. «مأجّل شراء سيارة هالسنة؟» reads household purchasing power off
   59,264 units, and it is the cleanest yes/no ask in the slate.
3. **The end-questions were deliberately de-duplicated.** Two of the five originally asked about
   salaries; the oil reel's was rewritten to petrol spend so no two reels in one evening ask the
   viewer the same thing. The five now ask about a salary, a rate paid, a weekly petrol spend, a
   deferred purchase, and a delivery time — five different pockets.
