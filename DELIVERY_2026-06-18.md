1. **iraq-mosul-museum** — The Mosul Cultural Museum (Iraq's 2nd-largest), ransacked by ISIS in 2015 and damaged in the 2017 battle, is set to reopen in autumn 2026 after a ~20-year closure and an 8-year restoration led by a 5-partner consortium — Musée du Louvre (conserving antiquities), ALIPH Foundation (funder), Smithsonian Institution, World Monuments Fund (architecture) and Iraq's State Board of Antiquities. A symbol of post-war cultural recovery (Mosul liberated 2017). Sources: Smithsonian Magazine, Musée du Louvre, ALIPH Foundation, The Times of Israel, The Art Newspaper.
2. **iran-us-deal-signing** — The US and Iran plan to sign an interim memorandum of understanding on Friday in Switzerland, opening the path to end the war that began 28 February 2026: Iran could access a $300B economic-development program after permanent-peace talks; the US pledges no new sanctions or additional forces; 60 days of talks to end the war and constrain the nuclear program; Tehran reaffirms it will not pursue nuclear weapons and freezes its program's status quo. Sources: Bloomberg, France 24, Council on Foreign Relations, Associated Press.
3. **mammal-regeneration** — Two studies in *Science* (team incl. Texas A&M) show mammals' ability to rebuild complex tissue is switched off, not lost: oxygen sensing suppresses regeneration in mammals (amphibians sustain it), and after injury mice fail to fully activate the gene Aldh1a2 (which makes retinoic acid, a vitamin-A derivative). Reactivating the switch or injecting retinoic acid let mice regrow ear-hole tissue. Preclinical — mice only, human trials still needed. Sources: Science (AAAS), ScienceDaily, Texas A&M, ScienceAlert, Nature.
4. **saudi-seha-hospital** — Saudi Arabia's Seha Virtual Hospital, the world's largest virtual hospital (launched 2022), now links 242 hospitals on one telemedicine platform under the Vision 2030 Health Sector Transformation Program; it delivered 16 million+ virtual appointments and consultations in 2025 (+56% vs 2024); service coverage reached 97.4% of the Kingdom; healthcare contributes ~5% of GDP; 30+ specialties; the Kingdom aims to export its digital-health / medical-AI model (Health Minister Fahad Al-Jalajel). Sources: Saudi MOH, Vision 2030, Arab News, Fast Company ME, ITIJ.
5. **jwst-exoplanet-roasted** — NASA's James Webb Space Telescope observed exoplanet HD 80606 b — 4× Jupiter's mass — on a highly elliptical 111-day orbit around a Sun-like star; its temperature surges ~1,100°F near periastron. Webb's MIRI instrument took spectroscopy before/during/after closest approach and detected methane (CH₄) and carbon dioxide (CO₂). Led by Tiffany Kataria (NASA JPL). Sources: NASA, NASA JPL, Space.com, Johns Hopkins APL.
6. **superconductor-leap** — Researchers at Chalmers University of Technology (Sweden), with RISE, showed that sculpting/nanofaceting the substrate beneath an ultrathin YBCO film keeps it superconducting at higher temperatures and stronger magnetic fields — the film is only a few nanometers thick (<1-millionth of a human hair) on an MgO substrate. The novelty: engineer the surface, not the material. Promises far more energy-efficient electronics and quantum tech, while ICT already uses 6–12% of global electricity. Led by Prof. Floriana Lombardi. Published in Nature Communications, 17 June 2026. Sources: ScienceDaily, Chalmers, Bioengineer, EurekAlert, Nature Communications.
