# Photonect NEWS Leap Spec — 2026-05-08

> Synthesis of 9 watch dossiers covering ~100 of the world's best news short-form videos (Vox, AJ Arabic, AJ English, BBC, Vice, Bloomberg/Insider, Reuters/NowThis, C4/Sky/France 24).

## What we learned

The reels that actually stop a thumb DO ten things our current NewsReel engine doesn't. They don't all do the same ten — but **every cluster shares at least 4** of these moves. Our reels currently do 0-1 of them. That's the leap.

## The 11 moves Photonect must adopt for May 8

Patterns ordered by **breadth-of-consensus** across the 9 dossiers, then by **implementation cost** (cheap wins first). Each move cites which clusters use it.

### 1. Cold open WITHOUT title or VO (5 clusters: BBC, Vice, Vox-a, Vox-b, Reuters/NowThis)
**The pattern.** First 0.5-3.5s is footage + audio only. No title card, no kicker chip, no logo, no narrator. The viewer has to ask "what is this?" before any text shows up.
**What we do now.** We open on a centered title card with kicker + headline.
**Implementation.** `Breaking.tsx`: rebuild as a 4-phase scene:
- 0.0-0.5s: hero image full-bleed at 80% darkness, no text
- 0.5-2.0s: ambient SFX fade-in (foley from photo: market hum / engine room / fluorescent buzz / etc.)
- 2.0-3.5s: hero image lifts to 100%, the kicker chip slides in lower-left ONLY (small, attribution style)
- 3.5-7.0s: headline reveals via masked phrase-by-phrase RTL wipe (we have this animation already — extend timing)
- 7.0s+: existing breaking scene continues

### 2. Strategic SILENCE drop at the climax beat (4 clusters: Vox-a, BBC, Vice, AJ Arabic)
**The pattern.** Music CUTS DEAD for 0.6-1.0s at the emotional pivot of the reel. Raw verité or single SFX naked underneath. Then music returns at a heightened intensity.
**What we do now.** Continuous music bed for the full 60s.
**Implementation.** `NewsReel.tsx` audio: at the climax frame of BeatC (the "what's next" moment, ~52s in), full-mute the music for 24 frames. Re-enter at 0.5x volume then ramp to 0.6.

### 3. Giant typographic stat-stamp at the climax (2 clusters but extreme leverage: Bloomberg/Insider, Vox-a)
**The pattern.** The reel's killer number isn't in a stat card — it's stamped 60-80% of frame width in white over the broll, at the climax frame. "$13.3B" / "$80" / "9 weeks". You can't miss it.
**What we do now.** bigStat is a small card in the corner with a label. The number is barely 8% of frame width.
**Implementation.** `BeatC.tsx`: at the bigStat reveal beat (~46-52s), the number scales from 0.6 to 1.0 with `spring()`, fills 60% of frame width, ivory white at 95% opacity, 900-weight Tajawal numerals. The `arabicLabel` slides in below at 12% frame width. Vignette pulse on entry.

### 4. Loop-hook engineering (final frame matches opener) (2 clusters: Bloomberg/Insider, BBC)
**The pattern.** The closing frame visually rhymes with frame 0. Music resolves to opening key. Cold-cut at the loop seam, NEVER fade — fade signals "over." Result: IG autoplay drops viewer back at 0:00 and the brain reads continuity.
**What we do now.** Sources slate ends abruptly with handle.
**Implementation.** `Sources.tsx`: final 2 seconds match `Breaking.tsx` opening. Same hero image, same vignette, same kicker chip position. Music: Suno tracks regenerated with ABA structure (last 2s resolves to opening 2s). Cold-cut at the very last frame.

### 5. ONE canonical Arabic headline sentence (not 3 variants) (1 cluster but ALL of AJ Arabic)
**The pattern.** AJ Arabic writes one sentence and reuses it as: headline + caption opener + ticker line 1. Photonect currently writes three different sentences for the same story.
**What we do now.** Three variants — copywriter rewrites for each surface.
**Implementation.** `iraqi-copywriter.md` rule update: "The arabicHeadline string is the SAME string used as caption.txt's first line and arabicTicker[0]. Never paraphrase across these three surfaces. One sentence, locked, repeated."

### 6. Persistent source-credit attribution chips (3 clusters: Vox-a, C4/Sky, Reuters/NowThis)
**The pattern.** In the lower-left corner, a small chip ALWAYS shows the source of the current broll: "REUTERS · MAY 6" / "WIKIMEDIA · CC-BY" / "AP · BAGHDAD". Credibility is a continuous design element, not an end-card afterthought.
**What we do now.** Sources only appear in the closing slate.
**Implementation.** Add a new component `SourceChip.tsx` that renders in the lower-left of every Beat scene. The Beat schema gets a `broll_source` field per beat. `NewsReel.tsx` wires it through.

### 7. Question-frame headlines + closing rhetorical question (2 clusters: C4/Sky, Bloomberg/Insider)
**The pattern.** Headline framed as "WHAT'S X'S ROLE?" / closing line is a rhetorical question for comment-bait. Photonect already does cliffhanger questions per beat — extend to the headline AND closing ticker line.
**What we do now.** Headline is declarative.
**Implementation.** `iraqi-copywriter.md`: arabicHeadline can take question form (one of three voice options); arabicTicker[6] (last line) MUST be a question.

### 8. Audio fade-in 1.5s before image (3 clusters: Vice, Vox-a, Bloomberg/Insider)
**The pattern.** Audio starts 1.5s before the picture cuts up from black. Viewer commits aurally before they even see the first frame.
**What we do now.** Audio + image start together at frame 0.
**Implementation.** `NewsReel.tsx` Audio component: shift the music start to frame 0 with the image starting at frame 45 (1.5s @ 30fps). Add a 0-45 frame `<AbsoluteFill>` of pure black behind everything.

### 9. White burned-in captions on translucent dark band (2 clusters: BBC, Vice)
**The pattern.** Sound-off design: a persistent bottom translucent dark band (~10% frame height) carries a single white sans-serif phrase. Two lines max. Phrase changes every 3-5s in sync with VO/voice.
**What we do now.** Beat headings are designed cards in the upper area. Bottom is empty.
**Implementation.** `Beat*.tsx` scenes: add a bottom `<SubtitleBar>` component anchored to bottom-90px-tall, 65% black, 30px padding. The Beat schema gets a `subtitle_phrases: string[]` field (3-4 phrases per beat with start/end frames). Phrases reveal phrase-by-phrase in sync with the beat narrative.

### 10. Map-as-stage for geography stories (3 clusters: Vox-a, Vox-b, Reuters/NowThis)
**The pattern.** For geo-anchored stories (Hormuz, Sudan, Levant), the MAP fills the frame. Numbers, arrows, place names type out INSIDE the map, not on side cards. Maps are the canvas, not a graphic asset.
**What we do now.** No map mode.
**Implementation.** New scene type `MapStage.tsx` (additive — only used when story has `useMapStage: true`). Takes a base map image (e.g., a desaturated MENA satellite) + a list of `{place, number, arrow_to}` pins. Pins type out one at a time, arrows draw via SVG strokeDashoffset. Used for May 8's Hormuz/Sudan/Iraq-pipeline-style stories.

### 11. Foreign-source audio + Arabic subtitle bar inversion (1 cluster: Vox-a)
**The pattern.** Vox keeps the original-language audio AND original chyron, then layers a styled subtitle. We can do the inverse: when sourcing English/foreign material, keep that audio for credibility + add an Arabic subtitle bar.
**What we do now.** N/A (we don't currently use source audio).
**Implementation.** Future. For May 8, skip — but build the subtitle-bar component (move 9) so this is one prop change away from working.

## Risks + decisions made

- **Risk: silence drop misreads as a render glitch.** Mitigated by keeping 1 channel of low ambient at -32dB during the silence (still feels intentional).
- **Risk: giant stat stamp overwhelms the broll behind.** Mitigated by 12px black drop-shadow + the stat is held only 1.5s before scaling back.
- **Risk: subtitle bar duplicates beat heading.** Decision: subtitle phrases are NOT the same as beat headings — they're shorter (5-9 words each) and break the 40-70-word body across 3-4 phrases that reveal in sync. Headings stay where they are.
- **Risk: source chip clutters the frame.** Mitigated by 11pt typography in 50% white + 32px from corner edges + 70% opacity. Visible at sound-off scroll, invisible at engaged-watch.
- **Risk: timeline stretches past 60s with new cold-open phase.** Decision: keep total duration at 60s (1800 frames). Steal 1.5s from current Sources slate (which is too long anyway) and 1.5s from BeatA (our weakest beat). Net: cold open gets +3s, total stays.
- **Decision: don't ship MapStage on May 8.** Build the component but only wire one slug into it (the geography-heaviest one). This way Trial 2 (if needed) has the option to enable it without rebuilding.

## What we're NOT adopting (and why)

- **Vector-mocked social UI** (Reuters/NowThis): cool but reads as "this is a meta video about social media" — wrong genre for our news positioning.
- **Removal-as-hook (4s pure black)**: too much; our shortest engagement window is the first 2-3s, can't afford 4s of nothing.
- **Sustained 20-30s single-voice holds** (C4/Sky): we're at 60s total — can't afford a 30s hold.
- **AJ Arabic Mubasher 24 parallel tweet column**: requires a real social-listening API; out of scope.
- **Pre-war sequel structure**: requires a real archive; we're 6 weeks old.
- **Two-tier lower-third (kicker + main)** (France 24): redundant with our existing kicker + headline.

## Acceptance criteria for Trial 1

- All 11 moves implemented or explicitly skipped per "What we're NOT adopting."
- Triple QA passes 6/6.
- Western-digit sweep passes.
- Density caps pass (headline 9-13w, beat body 40-70w, ≤3 stats, ticker 7×10-17w).
- Visual spot-check on rendered videos: cold open is footage + audio only for first 2s; bigStat is huge; one full silence drop is audible; sources chips persist in lower-left of every beat; subtitle bar persists in bottom 10%.

If trial fails on any of these, the critic agent surfaces the gap and Trial 2 fixes it.
