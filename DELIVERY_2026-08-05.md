## Directional shift vs 2026-08-04

1. **The dinar anchor inverted its own geography.** Every prior dinar reel treated Erbil as the
   *expensive* market (08-04: Erbil 152,900 vs Baghdad 152,250). Today Erbil sold *below* Baghdad's
   shops — 152,150 against 152,500. The reel is built on that reversal rather than on a headline
   rate move, and it explicitly compares Erbil against the Baghdad **shop** rate, not the bourse,
   because those are the two numbers a traveller actually chooses between.
2. **A new slug class: "the money you never see."** `naqd-central-bank` is the first reel built on
   the *composition* of a flow rather than its size — $5.857bn sold, but only $319m of it as cash.
   That reframes a macro statistic the channel has never touched into the «وين راحت؟» question that
   is the single best-performing hook pattern in the view data.
3. **Posting order became an editorial instrument.** Previous slates accepted whatever order the
   alphabet produced and then apologised for adjacent same-pillar pairs in this document. Today the
   slugs were *named to schedule* — the topic sequence was designed first and the identifiers written
   to produce it, which is why two slugs carry `current-` and `naqd-` prefixes.

## Warnings

1. **No P3 slug — pillar mix is P1×4 + P2×1, not the specified 2-3/1-2/1.** No P3 candidate (science,
   health, sport, culture with a real Iraq/MENA hook) cleared the bar today: the only sport story with
   emotional pull was Iraq's World Cup qualification, which is from **April**, and the gold-price story
   could not be verified same-day. Rather than pad the slate with a story that fails the LENS TEST — the
   exact category the view data shows losing (11-28 views) — the fifth slot went to the oil story, which
   at least carries a direct budget consequence. **Deliberate deviation, flagged rather than hidden.**
2. **No corruption / صولة الفجر slug.** The task asks for ≥1 per day while the saga is live. No
   corruption development inside the 24-hour window could be sourced: the most recent verifiable items
   (Talal al-Zoubaie's arrest, the $19M seizure) are from **July**. The accountability vein is instead
   carried by the minister slug — a parliamentary hearing on public money — but that is not the same
   thing and the streak is broken.
3. **`topicBucket` is `iraq_domestic` on all four V11 slugs.** The legacy 7-bucket field therefore reads
   as a diversity breach even though the viewer-facing rotation (kickers, and the interleaved posting
   order) holds. The field is a leftover from the pre-July bucket model that the P1/P2/P3 pillars
   replaced; it is inaccurate to retag a Kirkuk power-plant story as `gulf_regional` merely to satisfy
   it. **Recommend retiring or redefining `topicBucket` in a separate engine session.**
4. **Cross-slug reading risk (advisory).** Slot 4 says the CBI sells **$5.857bn a month**; slot 3 says
   monthly oil revenue does not exceed **2.5tn dinars (≈$1.9bn)**. These are different concepts —
   FX-window sales versus treasury receipts — and each is labelled, but posted 75 minutes apart they may
   be read as a contradiction. Separated by the oil reel in the running order to blunt this.
5. **Timing.** Research began ~14:10 Baghdad against a ~16:00 target; the slate was pushed ~17:10 after
   two QA gates and a mid-run reorder. Still comfortably ahead of the 18:00 first post, but the
   11:00 Baghdad start recommended in the memory notes was not met.

## ⚠️ V11 shipped ×3, not ×4 — Edge-TTS blip on the lead reel

`current-khormor-gas` was authored as V11 and **failed at the voice-over step**:

```
rendering 2026-08-05-current-khormor-gas as V11 (voiced)
engine=edge words_in=80
VO generation failed (HOLD): timing sanity failed: last word ends 13.037s but audio is 0.6s — HOLD
warning: V11 render failed (rc=1) — falling back to V10.1
```

Edge-TTS returned a **0.6-second audio file** for an 80-word script whose word timings ran to 13.0s.
The timing-sanity guard caught the mismatch and held, and `render-reel.sh` fell back to V10.1 — which is
exactly the designed behaviour ("a V11 hiccup must NEVER cost a reel"). **The guard did its job: it
refused to ship a reel with 0.6s of audio.** This is a transient TTS/network blip, not a content or
schema fault — the brief passed every validator, and it was the first slug rendered in the run, which is
consistent with a cold-start failure.

**Not re-rendered**, deliberately: the reel was already scheduled for 18:00 and that slot had passed by
the time the failure was identified, so re-rendering and re-posting risked a **duplicate publish** for a
purely cosmetic format difference. The shipped reel is QA-clean and its content is identical.

**Consequences to carry forward:**
- Today's A/B contributes **3 V11 + 2 V10.1** data points, not the intended 4 + 1. `oil-under-80` is the
  designed control; `current-khormor-gas` is an **unintended** V10.1 sample and should be tagged as such
  when the format comparison is next computed, or it will bias the V10 arm with a services story.
- Worth considering in a future engine session (not during production): a single Edge-TTS retry before
  the fallback triggers, since the guard already proves the failure is detectable and the audio is cheap
  to regenerate.
