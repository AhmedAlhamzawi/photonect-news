## Editorial process
Copy went through the Opus copywriter, then **two** Opus editorial gate passes (sequential, never
parallel — the gate reviews stale text otherwise). All 12 source articles were pre-saved as plain text
so the gate never had to fetch the web.

**Gate findings I applied:** 10 blockers from pass 1, 2 from pass 2.
- `d` — **«منصة أور» removed everywhere.** I had taken it from a WebSearch *summary* I never verified
  against the page; no source supports it. `هذا اليوم` dropped as a source with it. Pass 2 then caught
  that the deletion had not reached `englishSubhead`, which V10.1 renders.
- `e` — **creditor direction was inverted.** «مطالبات لوزارة المالية لدى المركزي» reads as the CBI owing
  the ministry; it is the reverse. Now «مطالبات على وزارة المالية لصالح البنك المركزي».
- `e` — **end question presupposed a salary delay the reel denies.** Marsoumi's argument is that the
  borrowing *is funding* salaries. Replaced with «راتبك من الحكومة لو من الخاص؟».
- `e` — 72.5tn pinned next to the 66% label so it cannot collide with the 8.74tn «حوالات خزينة».
- `b` — **the opener claimed the dollar "remained" below 160k.** Yesterday Baghdad shops were 160,250,
  so today is a crossing, not a hold. Neutral factual opener instead.
- `b` — **CBI rebuttal added** to the aired voText. A currency-redenomination rumour was airing as the
  lead driver of dollar buying with no official response, even though the CBI's on-record answer was
  sitting in our own source set.
- `a` — **Saturday facts were folded into an «اليوم الأحد» VO.** Both the Riyadh and transport-ministry
  sentences now carry «أمس السبت». On V11 only the VO airs, so a correct label in props is not enough.
- `a` — **«هالأسبوع» was invented.** The advisory carries no time window at all. Removed from hook, end
  question and caption.
- `c` — we compared 300 **days** to 24 **storms** in our own voice. Replaced with the source's own
  «حتى التسعينات…», which also fixes a TTS garble (spelled-out "1951–1990" is unspeakable).

**Gate findings I overrode, after verifying:**
