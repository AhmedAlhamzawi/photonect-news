## Directional shift vs 2026-08-05

1. **The dinar anchor stopped being a dinar reel.** Every prior anchor compared dollar rates against
   each other — bourse vs shop vs Erbil. Today's pairs the dollar against **gold**, because both rose
   on the same morning and they are the two instruments an Iraqi household actually stores savings in.
   The end question follows the format («تخزن فلوسك دولار لو ذهب؟») and is answerable in one word,
   which the abstract-opinion questions never were.
2. **Possessive headlines.** «دولارك يصعد… وذهبك يقفز وياه» and «37 يوم… وين راتبك؟» put the second-person
   pronoun in the hook itself rather than in the body. The view data says the winning reels are the ones
   whose *headline* answers «شنو راح يخسر؟» — this is the first slate where four of five headlines do that
   grammatically, not just thematically.
3. **Two services slugs, deliberately split by cause.** Electricity (a share being cut from above) and
   petrol (a quota being raised that changed nothing on the ground) are the same complaint from opposite
   directions. Running both, separated in the schedule by the salaries reel, tests whether the
   services vein sustains two slots a day or cannibalises itself.
