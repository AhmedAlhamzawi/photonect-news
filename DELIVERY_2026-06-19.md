Sources: ESPN, FIFA, FOX Sports, Yahoo Sports, SBS News.
3. **saudi-tourism-2030** — Saudi Arabia welcomed ~122 million visitors in 2025 (+5% YoY), including 29.7M international (+8%); tourism spending hit ~SR300 billion ($81B, +6%). After beating its 100M target six years early, the Kingdom raised its goal to 150M annual visitors by 2030 (70M international + 80M domestic), driven by NEOM, AlUla and the Red Sea project. Sources: Arab News, Saudi Ministry of Tourism, Vision 2030, Hotelier Middle East, Gulf News.
4. **preservatives-heart** — A French study (NutriNet-Santé, *European Heart Journal*) tracked ~112,000 adults for ~8 years and linked common food preservatives to higher blood pressure and cardiovascular disease: highest intake of non-antioxidant preservatives carried +29% hypertension risk and +16% cardiovascular risk; 8 preservatives were flagged (incl. sodium nitrite E250, potassium sorbate E202). Observational — association, not proven causation; more studies called for. Led by Dr Mathilde Touvier (INSERM). Sources: European Heart Journal, European Society of Cardiology, INSERM, ScienceDaily, CNN.
5. **stargate-uae-ai** — "Stargate UAE", an AI datacenter megaproject in Abu Dhabi, begins operations in 2026: first phase 200 MW running ~100,000 Nvidia GB300 chips; the ~26 km² (10 sq mi) site will eventually reach 5 GW, built by UAE firm G42 with US partners. The alliance includes OpenAI, Oracle, Nvidia, Cisco and Japan's SoftBank — positioning the UAE as a global AI hub. Sources: Nvidia, Reuters, Data Center Dynamics, Yahoo Finance, SemiAnalysis.
6. **amazon-fungus-spider** — An international team (incl. Germany's Leibniz Institute LIB) described a new spider, *Taczanowskia waska*, in the Ecuadorian Amazon — the first documented case of a spider mimicking a parasitic fungus, imitating the fruiting body of the spider-killing genus *Gibellula* in appearance and behavior. Found in the highly biodiverse Llanganates-Sangay Corridor; published in *Zootaxa*. Sources: Leibniz Institute LIB, ScienceDaily, Phys.org, SciTechDaily, CBC.
