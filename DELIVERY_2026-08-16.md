## Engine fix shipped with this slate

**The 08-15 slot-collision bug is fixed and covered by a test.** `post-to-uploadpost.py` picked its
posting slot with `slots[min(posted + skipped + failed, …)]`, but a *partial* platform failure
increments **both** `posted` and `failed` — so one half-failed post advanced the cursor by two. On
08-15 that skipped **19:45**, our single best slot, and ran the cursor off the end of the list,
**scheduling two reels at the same 23:45** where they self-competed.

reproduces the original failure shape (lead reel posts to X + Instagram, fails TikTok) and asserts
the schedule: **on the old code it produces `[NOW, 21:15, 22:30, 23:45, 23:45]` — 19:45 skipped and a
collision; on the fixed code `[NOW, 19:45, 21:15, 22:30, 23:45]`.** Both directions were run, so the
test is known not to be vacuous.

This is the distribution script, not the Remotion render engine — no engine code was touched.

### The two blockers

- **⚠ The lead reel contradicted our own feed, in our own voice.** The hook and the opening VO line
  read «إنتاجنا 30 ألف ميغاواط» — *our production is 30,000 MW* — stated as objective fact, first
  person, unattributed. But **our own 07-31 slate** (`2026-07-31-power-2tn-unused`) quotes Electricity
  Minister **Ali Wahib** telling parliament on **27 July** that production had **fallen to 22,000 MW**
  against a need of 60,000. Two reels on the same feed, twenty days apart, 22,000 → 30,000, with no
  explanation. The 30,000 is the PM's own figure *inside a directive* — almost certainly installed
  capacity, not the delivered production the minister described; treating the two as the same quantity
  was our error, not the source's. Every surface now carries the figure **inside the attribution**
  («لما وصفه التوجيه بالإنتاج الحالي») and never as our own claim. I verified the archive contradiction
  against the 07-31 props myself before acting on it.
  *This is the second slate running where the decisive catch came from checking our own archive rather
  than the source.*
- **⚠ The control reel presupposed that a currency change is under way.** Across three beats, a ticker
  and a caption, the reel repeated «أسباب تغيير العملة» / «آليات التنفيذ» / «المدد الزمنية» without
  ever saying that **no authority has confirmed any such decision exists**. The net takeaway from a
  23:45 dinar reel would have been *"Iraq is changing its currency and the government won't say why"* —
  on the one topic where a false takeaway moves real dinar/dollar behaviour. Beat 1 now opens by
  stating that neither the central bank nor the finance ministry has confirmed a decision, and the
  headline names the talk as talk. Note this is the **V10.1 control**, so props ship verbatim with no
  brief to soften them — and the engine does not render `arabicTicker` at all, so the correction had
  to go in a **beat body**, not the ticker.
