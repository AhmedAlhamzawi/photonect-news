- **A false claim in the largest on-screen text of the anchor reel.** The dollar hook read

  as a general statement: Baghdad's money-shops **sell at 153,500** while Erbil sells at **153,150**,
  so a buyer in Erbil actually pays **350 dinars less** per $100. The claim is true only against the
