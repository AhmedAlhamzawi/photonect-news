## Editorial QA — what the gates caught

An Opus copywriter authored the slate, then **two independent Opus editorial gates** ran over it
(facts/neutrality, and comprehension/render-safety). The facts gate re-derived every computed figure
independently and all nine reconciled exactly (350 · 1,000 · 400 · 50 · 150 · 62,000 · 23.69 ·
145+107=252 · 5×252=1,260). **All of its findings were applied in full.** The consequential ones:

- **A false claim in the largest on-screen text of the anchor reel.** The dollar hook read
  «الدولار بأربيل صار أغلى من بغداد» — *the dollar is dearer in Erbil than in Baghdad*. That is wrong
  as a general statement: Baghdad's money-shops **sell at 153,500** while Erbil sells at **153,150**,
  so a buyer in Erbil actually pays **350 dinars less** per $100. The claim is true only against the
  **bourse** reference rate (153,100), which is not a price anyone transacts at. Posting it would have
  invited the obvious reply — *"I paid 153,500 in Baghdad yesterday"* — on the channel's own anchor
  beat. Corrected on all four surfaces: hook → «الدولار بأربيل فوق بورصة بغداد», the spoken VO
  («متجاوزاً **بورصة** بغداد»), the headline, and the caption body, which now names the shop price too.
  This is the second slate running where the bourse/shop distinction produced the run's worst defect.
- **An invented statistic.** The security reel's beat-2 bigStat was a bare **«3»** — the author's own
  count of agenda headings in a quoted sentence, a count that yields 4 just as easily. Shafaq never
  published such a figure. Replaced with a non-numeric «الحدود» naming the first stated agenda item.
- **A fabricated disbursement timeline.** The hospitals English subhead said **"$1.26BN TRANSFERRED
  SINCE 2011"**. The source gives two separate facts — $1.26bn transferred cumulatively, and referral
  to investment in 2011 — and never links them; indeed $107m per hospital was added in **2020**. Split
  into two clauses.
- **Date drift on the oil caption.** «سعر خام البصرة **اليوم**» presented the final session's close as
  today's price on a Saturday week-wrap. Now «خام البصرة ينهي الأسبوع».
- **A grade conflation.** «خام البصرة ربح 13.29%» attached Basrah *Medium's* weekly gain to both
  grades (Heavy gained 12.03%). Now «البصرة المتوسط».
- **A live allegation asserted without a source chip.** The hospitals pill row stated flatly that the
  company is «غير مسجّلة» with «لا يوجد» branch — true as al-Issawi's characterisation, but the pill
  surface carried no attribution while a committee has only *opened a file*. Both pills now read
  «(العيساوي)».
- **Words put in a named official's mouth.** Article 112 was rendered as al-Abd Rabbuh's quote; the
  source lists it as a fact of the article. Re-scoped, and the ticker line de-attributed.
- **A negative finding credited to an outlet that only carried the story.** «بلا قرارات معلنة … بحسب
  شفق نيوز ووكالة بغداد اليوم» — Baghdad Today carried the meeting, not the absence of decisions.
  Now attributed to Shafaq alone; Baghdad Today stays in the sources line, where it is legitimate.
- Plus «محتسب» added to the two Nineveh hook/headline surfaces that were carrying a computed figure bare.

**All 8 statPop anchors were re-verified after the edits** — each `matchWord` still appears exactly
once in its own `voText`, still adjacent to its number, and **every voText contains zero commas**, so
the 08-13 karaoke word-join bug is avoided by construction rather than by luck.

The second gate (comprehension/render-safety) then found a **larger** error the first had missed, plus
five more. All were applied:

- **⚠ The reel's entire premise was false — and our own archive disproved it.** The slate was built on

- **The 350 was spoken with no direction.** «بفارق محتسب مقداره ثلاثمئة وخمسين ديناراً» — a difference

- **A U+2212 MINUS SIGN** in a tanker stat pill — the only one in the slate, and a tofu/bidi-flip risk
