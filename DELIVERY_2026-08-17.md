### The blockers

  See Media above. This is the first time a gate on this channel killed an image rather than a
  sentence, and it is the finding I most should have caught myself: I had Read-verified that frame
  and passed it, because I was checking whether it was *authentically Iraqi*, not whether it was
  *defamatory*.
- **⚠ The lead reel carried a one-word guilty verdict.** The kicker chip read **فساد** over a named
  man a court has not ruled on, and the hook stated the seizure flat in our own voice with no source.
  Now kicker **نزاهة** (the institution, not a verdict) and the hook names النزاهة inside the
  sentence. This is the third occurrence of this exact failure mode on this channel.
- **⚠ The lead reel insinuated a causal link it cannot support.** Nothing in the Commission statement
  ties this man to anyone's outage hours, yet a beat spelled out the bridge («أي المسار الذي تصل
  عبره ساعات الكهرباء إلى المنازل») and the reel closed on «شكد ساعة كهرباء وصلتك اليوم؟». The bridge
  is deleted and the end question is now «سمعت بحملة النزاهة قبل اليوم لو لا؟» — still personal and
  one-word-answerable, but it no longer asks the viewer to blame a specific man for their power cuts.
- **⚠ The dinar reel compared a shop price to a bourse price — and published the mismatch as our own
  arithmetic.** Erbil's 154,000 is a **selling** price; Baghdad's 154,000 is a **bourse** price. We
  had framed them as equal and tagged it «(محتسب)», certifying a category error as computation. The
  honest comparison is Erbil 154,000 against Baghdad's **shop** 154,500 — **Erbil is 500 dinars
  cheaper**, the opposite of the framing. Fixed in the VO, the beat, the pill set, the ticker and the
  caption. *Note this same shop-vs-bourse mismatch sits in yesterday's 08-16 reel («+50 Erbil
  premium») — flagged for the next engine session.*
- **⚠ The ration reel's hook manufactured a national rice shortage.** «تايلاند: صفر رز للعراق» drops
  «منها» and reads as *Iraq is getting no rice*, which the reel's own body contradicts two sentences
  later. Now «رز البطاقة.. صفر من تايلاند والتجارة تقول كافي» — both claims, both attributed.
- **⚠ The Kurdistan reel told employees they had been paid.** «رواتب تموز وصلت» asserts payment;
  the ministry announced only that **funding was deposited**, with the payout table still pending —
  the reel's own closing line says so. Now «تمويل رواتب تموز وصل.. ومتأخرات المتقاعدين لا». The
  withheld ~3.327bn is also now **spoken as a figure**, because unquantified «المبلغ المخصص» after
  an 830-billion pop makes a listener scale it as *all* retiree pay, when it is back-pay for
  earlier months.
