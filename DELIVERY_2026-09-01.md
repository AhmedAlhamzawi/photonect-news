## Both Opus gates returned DO-NOT-SHIP

Two independent gates ran (one on numbers/attribution, one on what the image+words assert together). Combined they raised **21 blockers**; all were applied before render. The ones that mattered:

**Image + text pairs publishing claims no source made** — the recurring failure class, and the reason both gates blocked:

1. **The lead reel's hero was the wrong aircraft entirely.** A Russian Mi-17 sat under the headline «مروحيات أميركية» — telling a fast viewer both that these are the American helicopters and that Iraq already flies them. The story is a *possible* sale, not a delivery. Replaced with a clean, unmarked, factory-fresh light twin on a manufacturer ramp.
2. **A 1960s Huey with its cowlings open under the $800M price tag** — implying the money buys worn, torn-down airframes. Replaced.
3. **A derelict Mi-17, weeds through the undercarriage,** under the line about replacing the Mi-17 fleet. The sources say only that *spare parts* got hard to source; the frame published an editorial verdict that Iraq's fleet is junk. Swapped for the in-service Mi-17 on an active apron — which is exactly what that beat needed.
4. **A photorealistic prison cell under two named, living men.** V11 draws no «صورة توضيحية» chip, so the frame reads as documentary and asserted their current incarceration and conditions — which no source establishes. Cut from the slate entirely and replaced with a neutral government building.
5. **An empty government payout hall, every window unstaffed,** paired with a question about "this month's" salary — together asserting a present salary stoppage the record does not support. Replaced with an oil-export loading-arm frame, and the question re-anchored to July (below).

**End-question audit** — the metric that has burned this channel twice more:

6. The lead reel spent its whole runtime establishing that **nothing has been spent** (`bigStat: 0`, «عقد موقّع: لا»), then asked viewers where they'd spend the $800M "in your area" — handing back as committed money the very expenditure the reel had just denied, with a guns-vs-butter frame no source supports.
