## Story signals & sources
1. **iraq-rice-rebound** — Iraq's water storage rebounded to ~**30 billion m³** in 2026 (from ~**4.5 billion** the prior year) after heavy rains and stronger Tigris–Euphrates flows; the Agriculture Ministry approved ~**362,000 dunums** of rice — the largest area in **4 years**. Sources: Arab News, Iraqi Agriculture Ministry, Reuters, FAO, UNICEF.

Sources: Al Jazeera, US State Department, Reuters, CNN, The National.
3. **europe-heat-dome** — An Omega-block "heat dome" over Western Europe pushes **43–45°C** across central/western France, a possible first-ever June **40°C** in the UK, and **42–45°C** in Spain — **12–18°C** above seasonal norms — with wildfire/drought warnings; the WMO warns the next **5 years** could be the hottest on record. Sources: Severe Weather Europe (ECMWF/GFS), WMO, Met Office, Météo-France, PBS.
4. **clinical-ai-benchmark** — A **Nature Medicine** study published **June 23** found general-purpose LLMs (**GPT-5.2, Gemini 3.1 Pro, Claude Opus 4.6**) outperformed two **FDA-cleared** clinical AI tools (OpenEvidence, UpToDate Expert AI) across every benchmark — **500** MedQA + **500** HealthBench + **100** real clinical queries, **12** US clinicians, **1,800** blinded annotations — exposing a regulatory validation gap relevant to Gulf health systems. Sources: Nature Medicine, Clinical Trial Vanguard, Digg, Science, npj Digital Medicine.
5. **meteorite-lost-world** — The angrite meteorite **NWA 12774**, found in the Sahara, preserves evidence of a lost early protoplanet (radius **1,000–1,800 km** — moon-to-Mars sized) that orbited the young Sun ~**4.56 billion years** ago then shattered; aluminum-rich clinopyroxene required ≥**17.5 kilobars** of pressure, only possible inside a massive body. Lead: Aaron Bell, University of Colorado Boulder; published in *Earth and Planetary Science Letters*. Sources: ScienceDaily, Phys.org, Sci.News, SciTechDaily, Earth.com.
