## Opus editorial QA gate — 4 blockers + 13 fixes applied before render

**BLOCKERS**

1. **Synthetic images were credited to real newsrooms.** All 15 beats carried `brollSource` values like
   `"Reuters · Bloomberg · 2026"`, `"Integrity Commission · 2026"`, `"Security Media Cell · 2026"`.
   `schema.ts` documents that field as a persistent **photo-credit chip** (its own examples are
   `"REUTERS · MAY 6"`, `"WIKIMEDIA · CC-BY"`) and `Beat.tsx` renders it over every beat — while
   `media-stamp.json` records 100% of the imagery as generated. That is misattribution to real
   organisations. → now **«صورة توضيحية · Photonect AI»** slate-wide. Claim attribution is untouched
   (it lives in `arabicBody`, `bigStat.arabicLabel`, the ticker and the Sources scene).
   **This is systemic — prior slates carry the same false credits.**
2. **A false equation on screen.** `«… 26.946 تريليون من أصل 33.747 تريليوناً، أي 84%»` — that quotient is
   **79.85%**, not 84%. Sources give both claims and they do not reconcile, so the copy must not weld
   them. The total was removed from the body; 84% now stands alone as a sourced bigStat.
3. **Wrong weekday.** `«الثلاثاء 22 تموز»` — 22 July 2026 is a **Wednesday**. Sources conflict, so the
   weekday was dropped rather than guessed.
4. **The picture asserted a fact the copy deliberately omitted.** Beat 1's evidence shot showed **gold
   bars**, implying the unreconciled "400 kg of gold" cumulative that the script intentionally leaves out.
   Reshot cash-only.
